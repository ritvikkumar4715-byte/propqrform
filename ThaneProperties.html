Avatara: The Forest Kingdom

Luxurious 2 & 3 BHK Starts At ₹ 89 Lacs Onwards

In Front of Government School No. 125, Behind Muktidham Mandir, Datta Mandir Road, Nashik Road, Nashik


Developer: ACE Realty

Status: Under Construction

Expected Possession Date: 23rd Sep 2031

RERA Status: Approved

Price Range: ₹89 lakh - ₹1.60 crore

Project Area: 8.5 Acre

No. of Floors: G+ 47 Storey Towers


Avatara The Forest Kingdom is a green and serene residential development coming up near Ghodbunder Road, Thane. This 2-tower-strong landmark will feature premium 2 BHK and 3 BHK residences overlooking the greenest part of Thane and the pristine Thane Creek. Each home in this enclave has been royally designed to offer a pure blend of indulgence and bliss. The Greendom Club and 50+ curated amenities, including a mini theatre, banquet hall, business centre, workpods, indoor games zone, gymnasium, badminton court, aerobics & yoga room, pickleball court, multipurpose court, cricket turf, and miyawaki plantation, make it a resident’s paradise.

Situated in the greenest hub of Thane, Avatara The Forest Kingdom is minutes away from amenities such as Podar International School, Hiranandani School, AP Shah Institute of Technology, Tieten Medicity Hospital, Hiranandani Hospital, Horizon Prime Hospital, TCS Campus, R Mall, Hypercity Mall, and D-Mart. The Anandnagar Vadavali Road connects this enclave with Ghodbunder Road and, through it, to important navigation corridors. The Kasarvadavali Metro Station on Line 4 is just 2 minutes away. The Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport is just a 1-hour drive away. So, come and live an aspirational life at Avatara The Forest Kingdom in Thane.
