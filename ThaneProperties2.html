Piramal Vaikunth: Vidhaan

Luxurious 2 & 3 BHK Homes Starts from ₹ 1.57 Cr Onwards

Old Mumbai - Agra Road, Balkum Naka Balkum, Thane West, Thane, Maharashtra


Developer: Piramal Realty

Status: Phase 2 Under Construction

Expected Possession Date: Oct 2032

RERA Status: Approved

Price Range: ₹1.59 Cr - ₹3.34 Cr onwards

Project Area: 32 Acres

No. of Floors: 51 Storeys

Carpet Area: 719 - 1,049 sq. ft.


With a remarkable response to phase 1, Piramal Vaikunth is launching its phase 2, a flourishing community amid the sprawling natural landscapes in Balkum, Thane. The stunning elevations comprise 2 BHK & 3 BHK apartments with a designer modular kitchen with smooth finishings. Exclusive Digi-Select 2 BHK homes are equipped with motion sensors, Amazon Echo, smart door locks, temperature & lighting control, etc. The spacious balconies of your residences treat you to mesmerising views of the Thane Creek and Yeoor Hills. This mixed-use development features around 60,000 sq. ft. of amenities space, including 40% of open space. Moreover, you can access exclusive amenities like an ISKCON temple within the premises, treehouses, an amphitheatre, a swimming pool, a pet park, a cafe, a yoga zone, etc.

Situated in the booming neighbourhood of Thane, your residence is close to the city's top facilities. It includes DAV Public School, C P Goenka International School, Jupiter Hospital, Narayan e-Techno School, Highland Super Speciality Hospital, R Mall, Viviana Mall, Dmart, NaMo Grand Central Park and more. There is an upcoming Balkum metro station within 5 minutes of reach. You will also be close to the Eastern Express Highway, LBS Marg and Ghodbunder Road. Thane railway station is also easy to access from here. So, Piramal Vaikunth presents an appealing community in the thriving neighbourhood of Balkum, Thane.
