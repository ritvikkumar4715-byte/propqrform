Luxurious Studio, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 3.5, 4 and 5 BHK configurations, varying by tower and phase

Status: 4 towers delivered; Raynav topped out and under construction; Rayansh under construction

Expected Possession Date: Tower 5 Raynav: November 2028; Tower 6 Rayansh: July 2030

Piramal Revanta presents an evolved style of contemporary living in the new cultural, financial, and commercial heart of Mumbai – Mulund.
