Estella at Kalpataru Park City is an exquisite residential cluster in Thane. The newly launched towers J and K comprise 2 BHK, 3 BHK and 4 BHK apartments with huge decks. The upcoming metro station will ease the travelling for residents. So, own this prestigious address by Kalpataru Park City for a promising future in Thane.
